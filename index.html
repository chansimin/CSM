How to earn XP

  • Log a meal: +10 (up to 4 a day)
  • Hit your protein goal: +30
  • Hit your fibre goal: +15
  • Paddle or train: +20
  • 15-min walk or cycle: +15
  • Stay inside your calorie range: +15
  • Saturated fat under limit: +10
  • Sugar under limit: +10
  • Carbs under the daily limit: +10

The range counts once you’ve logged 3 meals, or at the end of the day. Eating too little doesn’t earn points. Kopi needs fuel too.
